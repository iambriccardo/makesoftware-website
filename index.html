Manifesto / Make Software

Computers are toys again.

Make Software is a community for people who want to create with computers the way others paint, write, or play music. Builders, creatives, and curious minds are welcome here.

We make software without needing to justify it through profit, scale, business models, or users. We want computers to feel magical, personal, and fun again. The work matters more than the image of the work. Broken demos, ugly code, and unfinished ideas are welcome.

AI with authorship
Use AI to explore, build, and move faster, but keep your own taste, judgment, and intention.
Small software is beautiful
A tool for five friends, a tiny game, a weird script, or a personal website can matter deeply.
Bring back the local internet
Less algorithm, more room. Less feed, more presence. Less audience, more peers.
Finish tiny things
The goal is not to build an empire in one night, but to leave with something that exists.
Weird is good
Software can be poetic, funny, nostalgic, useless, chaotic, personal, awkward, or absurd.
Taste over hype
When execution becomes cheap, taste matters more. We care about what feels alive.
Learn by making near others
Knowledge spreads faster when people sit next to each other, debug together, and share what they discover.
No gatekeeping
Beginners, experts, designers, engineers, artists, and tinkerers all belong here. Curiosity is enough.

For playful software, tiny finished things, and strange ideas made near others.
