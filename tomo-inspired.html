Manifesto / Make Software

Computers are toys again.

Make Software is a community for people who want to create with computers the way others paint, write, or play music.

Broken demos, ugly code, unfinished ideas, tiny tools, and strange scripts are welcome.

Values

What we care about.

AI with authorship Use AI fast, keep taste and intention.
Small software A tool for five friends can matter deeply.
Local internet Less feed, more peers and presence.
Finish tiny things Leave with something that exists.
Weird is good Poetic, funny, useless, awkward, alive.
No gatekeeping Beginners, experts, artists, tinkerers belong.
